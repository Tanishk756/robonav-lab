# Community-specific drafts

## Show HN preparation checklist

HN's current guidelines prohibit generated or AI-edited post/comment text. Write the actual submission and comments yourself; this file intentionally supplies no copy-ready HN text.

Facts to consider explaining in your own words: what you personally built and understand, why planning/control tradeoffs interest you, how someone can try the working tool without an account, which assumptions are idealized, and what technical feedback would help. Be available to discuss your code and experience. Use the working application URL rather than a sign-up page. Do not coordinate votes or ask friends to promote the submission. A routine version announcement alone may not qualify as a new Show HN.

Read [Show HN guidance](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) and [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) yourself before submitting. The maintainer should decide whether the project and their personal involvement meet the site's criteria.
